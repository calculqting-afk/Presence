# Attendance alerts

Two automatic features run behind the student dashboard. There is no Attendance reminders settings card or checkbox in Events:

- In-app alerts (always enabled): visible banner while Presence is open, with an Open Events action. No browser permission or server write. Checked every 15 seconds and on focus/data changes. No alert after the session expires. Old checkbox preferences no longer control this automatic behavior.
- Android phone notifications (APK only): automatically scheduled when Android notification permission is granted. On first eligible upcoming reminder in the foreground, an unprompted APK asks for OS permission once. Denied/rationale states are not repeatedly prompted; users can change permission in Android Settings. Prompt history is device-local. Permission denial does not disable in-app alerts. Works without a hosted push sender or Firebase Functions. Not remote push and not a guaranteed alarm. The website never requests phone/browser notification permission or schedules background notifications.

Each session has an opening reminder at `openAt` for an eligible student without a check-in, or at `closeAt` for a student with a check-in but no checkout. After a saved check-in, outstanding check-in reminders are replaced with checkout reminders. In-app opening banners are eligible only for two minutes after opening (or until the session closes, whichever is sooner), so reopening long after the start does not replay an old opening. Phone opening notifications must be scheduled from an earlier sync; they are not retroactively fired after their start time. Messages include the actual scheduled time because Android can delay delivery.

Cutoff reminders remain 10 and 5 minutes before `checkOutClosesAt`, per real session event ID. Thresholds at/before the action opens are skipped, so an opening and cutoff warning at the same instant do not create duplicate phone alerts (a window of five minutes or less has only its opening alert). This is intentional: the current AttendancePolicy permits late check-in until this final cutoff, not just until the on-time/late threshold. Completed attendance receives none. Checkout reminders preserve access for previously checked-in students even if their section was removed. Checkout-only reopened events never invite a new check-in or replay a past checkout opening.

Only active students with synced event and attendance snapshots are scheduled. Morning and afternoon stay separate. Notification permission applies to the installed app/device; pending reminders are scoped to the current account and cleared on logout. Event and attendance listeners stay active across student views, which can add reads when schedules/attendance change; no reminder documents are written to Firestore. No rules deployment or service account is required.

Android uses inexact notifications without requesting special exact-alarm access. Battery saving, force-stop, denied permissions and OS policies can delay/prevent delivery. Future reminders are bounded to the nearest 60, replenished when the app syncs. A new APK containing the plugin is required; a website update alone cannot add native support to an already installed APK.

Local reminders cannot observe remote edits, deletion, eligibility changes, or attendance completed on another device while the app is closed. Reopen/sync to cancel or reschedule them. Notification content includes the event title (potentially visible on the lock screen), but not student names/IDs/reasons. Logout cancels pending and delivered attendance notifications; opening the login page also clears them. Closing/backgrounding the app intentionally keeps scheduled reminders.

## Manual checks

1. Web: use an active eligible student with a future check-in start. Verify Events has no reminder settings card or checkboxes and no browser notification permission prompt appears. Keep Presence open at the start and verify the Check-in is open banner. Check in, then verify Checkout is open at its scheduled start. Verify the 10/5-minute cutoff banners only for unfinished actions. Complete checkout and verify no further banner.
2. Repeat for non-eligible, inactive, completed, missing/invalid schedule and checkout-only reopened sessions. No new-check-in reminders should appear. Verify separate morning/afternoon IDs.
3. Android: build/sync the updated APK, install, sign in and sync an eligible future reminder. Allow the one-time OS permission prompt. Background the app before check-in opens and verify the phone opening notification. After checking in and syncing, background before checkout opens and verify its phone notification. Also verify cutoff reminders. Timing may be delayed. Tap the notification to open Presence; Events provides attendance actions. If permission is denied, verify in-app reminders still work and the app does not repeatedly prompt. No eligible upcoming reminder or a background-only app must not trigger a permission prompt.
4. While open, change the cutoff, remove an event, complete checkout, revoke notification permission in Android Settings, or log out. Confirm stale pending notifications are removed when synced. Log into another account and verify no previous-account reminders remain. Logout while a permission prompt is pending must not reschedule old-account reminders.
5. Change the schedule from another device while the phone app is closed. Confirm the documented limitation: existing local reminders may remain until the phone app is reopened and synced. No claim of background server synchronization is made.

Automated tests cover opening timestamps, schedule edits, expiry, opening/cutoff collision prevention, policy, native reconciliation, automatic permission gating/denial, no web permission prompts, no settings-card dependency, banner lifecycle and logout queue ordering; physical phone delivery still requires device testing. Opening reminders and automatic UI-free behavior are included in APK v1.8.5; they do not change older installed APKs. No immediate newly-created-event push or paid Firebase setup is introduced.
