Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026 · Version 2026-11-01-v1

Information and purpose

Presence uses account/profile details, attendance, correction requests, surveys, fines/service records and audit history to manage accounts, verify attendance, prepare reports, process requests and protect the system. Features may also use submitted face photos, location/accuracy, session details, notification tokens and usage analytics.

Access and storage

Authorized roles access records needed for their duties and may export PDF/Excel reports. Services include Firebase, Google Drive/Apps Script, Cloudflare and GitHub; providers may process data outside the Philippines. Submitted face photos are uploaded for storage; a camera preview alone is not an upload.

Permissions and devices

Camera, location and notification permissions are requested separately. This acknowledgement does not grant them. Location is used for requested verification, without background location permission. Notifications may appear on your lock screen.

Sign-in preferences, settings and limited session data may remain on your device. Sign out on shared devices. Clearing app data does not delete server records.

Retention and your choices

Records and submitted photos currently have no automatic deletion schedule. Request access, correction, account closure, photo removal or a deletion review through the contact below. Audit history, disputes, provider copies and exports may need separate review. Identity verification may be required.

Security and updates

Authentication, role checks and database rules restrict access, but no system is risk-free. Agreement versions and dates are saved to your account. Important policy changes may require acknowledgement again.

Questions or privacy requests? Contact Calculqting on Facebook. Never send your password.
